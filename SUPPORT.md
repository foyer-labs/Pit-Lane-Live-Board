# Support

*[Italiano](SUPPORT.it.md)*

Pit Lane Live Board is the personal, non-commercial project of one person, published as
Foyer Labs. Questions and reports are answered as time allows, **with no promise** of a
reply, a fix or a timeline.

## Before asking

- Read the guide, especially *Troubleshooting* and the FAQ (available with the first
  release).
- Look in *Settings → Repairs* for a Pit Lane Live Board notice.
- Remember that the live data comes from unofficial F1 feeds: when F1 changes them,
  things can stop working for everyone at once. Search the
  [issues](https://github.com/foyer-labs/Pit-Lane-Live-Board/issues) before opening a new
  one.

## How to ask

Open an [issue](https://github.com/foyer-labs/Pit-Lane-Live-Board/issues/new/choose) with
the *Problem* or *Idea* form. English and Italian are both fine. For a problem, include:
- the Home Assistant and integration versions;
- the session (e.g. *2026 Italian GP, Race*);
- what you expected;
- what happened;
- if there is one, the Home Assistant log filtered on `pit_lane_live_board`.

**Never paste your F1TV token** in an issue, a log or a screenshot.

An issue labelled `needs info` with no reply from its author for two weeks is closed; it
can always be reopened.

A Buy Me a Coffee donation is a thank-you and buys neither support nor priority.
